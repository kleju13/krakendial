THEME

BACKGROUND

Hover to see it move. Backgrounds speed up while the graphics card is busy.

OWN BACKGROUNDS

A video, GIF or picture. Moving ones are cropped square and shrunk to 640×640. Theme tint dims and recolours the picture so the numbers stay readable; Original leaves it alone and puts a soft halo behind the text instead; ASCII redraws it as characters in the theme’s colours. Video costs more CPU than a drawn background.

BACKGROUND FRAME RATE

CAM captures every changed frame and sends it to the display, which costs CPU. A slow background already looks smooth at 10 fps.
